Trophy System Explorer

Clash Royale's Trophy Road, rebuilt in our language. The idea, not the art: every solved problem pays trophies, weighted by difficulty, and the running total walks you along a road of leagues. Unlike the 9 gem ranks — which are a pure function of solve count and can only go up — trophies are an economy: bonuses for closed rings and live streaks, optional decay when you go dark. Four ways it could look as the You-tab rank card, each with the earn table it assumes.

Everything here is inline SVG + CSS, no external assets — the trophy glyph, the gems and the road are all pure functions of (trophies, ranks), so they port to react-native-svg one element at a time. The road is the only new layout primitive: a horizontal ScrollView with equal-pitch league markers and a fill that interpolates within the current segment, not across the whole range — that is what keeps the early leagues from collapsing into a pixel.
