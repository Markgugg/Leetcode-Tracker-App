1Current app — recreated from source, with audit
The core problem

You've shipped a lot of surface area — feed, chat, radar, heatmap, pathways, AI interview, two parallel rank systems — but there is no answer to "what do I do right now?" The app opens on other people's activity. A user with no group and no solves sees an empty list and a tab bar.

WHOOP and Apple Fitness both open on you, today, one number. Everything else is a drill-down. That's the single biggest change.

You have three ranking systems
  • TIERS in profile.tsx — 10 meme tiers (Homeless → One Piece)
  • RANKS in ranks-data.ts — 9 gems (Bronze → Grandmaster)
  • POWER_RANKS in log.tsx — 8 more (Unranked → Legendary)

All three are driven by the same input (solve count). Two of them are dead weight — the profile screen even computes tier, nextTier, tierProgress, toNext and then only renders the gem. Keep one.

What to fix, in priority order
  1. Home becomes Today. Your ring, your streak, your next problem — friends below the fold.
  2. Kill two of three rank systems. Gems survive; memes become optional flair, power level dies.
  3. Rebuild onboarding as five paced steps with a payoff screen before the password wall.
  4. Fix the Pathways tag mismatch — it currently renders 0/0 for every topic.
  5. Surface or delete leaderboard + pathways; no href:null screens.
  6. Reactions on solves. A read-only social feed has no loop.
  7. Multi-group support — .limit(1) caps your most viral surface.
  8. Move settings out of Profile into a gear sheet.
  9. Replace every Alert.alert with inline state.
  10. Stats needs a verdict — one weakest topic, one action, then the charts.
