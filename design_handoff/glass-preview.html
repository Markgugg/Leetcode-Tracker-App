LeetAI — Liquid Glass tab bar

This is a browser simulation of a native material. iOS 26's Liquid Glass is a GPU material with real-time lensing, adaptive contrast and dynamic highlights. Nothing on the web can reproduce it exactly. What you see here approximates it honestly: a live, displaced copy of the backdrop (SVG feImage → feDisplacementMap lens map, bulging at the edges), plus blur, saturation lift, an edge rim and a specular sheen — the same recipe, done in software. Best in Chromium. Scroll the screens and tap the tabs — the bar refracts what moves under it, and the pill refracts as it travels.
